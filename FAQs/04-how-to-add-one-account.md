# how to add one account

## step-by-step

1. Open TBS.
2. Click **Add account** on the left side.
3. Fill in:
   - **Server Url**
   - **Nickname** (display name for this account)
4. In the access area, fill:
   - **Username** (username#tag or email)
   - **Password**
   - Proxy fields (only if you use proxy)
5. Click **Add access**.
6. Confirm your access appears in the table.
7. Click **Add account** at the bottom.

## tips

- Server URL should be a full URL (for example: `https://ts1.x1.international.travian.com`).
- You must have at least one access row before saving the account.

# why i cannot delete an account

You can delete an account only when it is **offline**.

If the account is online, paused, or still changing state, TBS will block delete to protect running data.

## step-by-step to fix

1. Select the account.
2. Click **Logout**.
3. Wait until the account is fully offline.
4. Click **Delete**.
5. Confirm the delete message.

## tip

If delete still fails, wait a few seconds and try again after status updates.

# how to login, pause, resume, restart, and logout an account

## step-by-step

1. In the left account list, click the account you want.
2. Click **Login** to open browser and start bot actions.
3. Click **Pause** to pause the account.
4. Click **Pause** again to resume (button changes between pause/resume behavior).
5. Click **Restart** only when account is paused.
6. Click **Logout** to close browser and set account offline.

## important behavior

- **Restart** works when the account is paused.
- If account is in a transition state (starting/pausing/stopping), wait a moment and try again.
- Always select an account first.

# what do village settings mean

## upgrade settings

- **Use hero resources when lack of resources**: use hero inventory resources when short.
- **Use Roman queue logic**: apply Roman-specific queue behavior.
- **Use complete immediately...**: use instant complete button when queue time is over your limit.
- **Use special upgrade method**: use alternate upgrade click path.

## auto refresh

- **Enable auto refresh**: refresh village page automatically.
- **Refresh every**: refresh interval range.

## npc crop exchange settings

- **Enable NPC Crop exchange**: turn on auto NPC exchange.
- **Despite warehouse overflow**: allow exchange even near overflow.
- **Granary %**: crop storage percent target.
- **Ratio**: how to split resources in exchange.

## feature settings

- **Enable auto claim village's quest**: auto claim village quest rewards.

## troop training settings

- **Enable train troop**: turn on automatic troop training.
- **Next train troop execute after**: training repeat delay.
- **Train maximum number if low on resource**: adjust training behavior when resources are low.
- Troop selectors and amount fields decide what troop to train and how many.
